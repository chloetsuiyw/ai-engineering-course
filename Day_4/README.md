# Session 04 - Exploratory Data Analysis and Visualisation

## Objective
Use EDA to generate modelling hypotheses and communicate findings.

## Dataset
A workplace training dataset tracking employee engagement, assessment scores, and training completion across departments.

## Key findings
1. Engagement score is strongly linked to training completion.
2. Assessment scores vary by department, suggesting uneven training experiences.
3. Completion rates differ significantly across departments.

## Modelling hypotheses
- Feature likely useful: engagement_score
- Feature likely useful: assessment_score
- Potential leakage risk: completion_days
